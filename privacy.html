Privacy & Cookie Notice

Last updated September 2026

What we collect

When you send us a message through the contact form, we receive the name, email address, phone number, project type and message you type in. We use that information to reply to you, to arrange a consultation, and to prepare an estimate. We do not sell it and we do not pass it to anyone outside Redux LLC and the software we use to manage projects.

Cookies

A cookie is a small piece of text this website asks your browser to keep. We use them in two ways, and only after you press Accept cookies on the banner:

  • redux_consent — remembers whether you accepted or declined, so you are not asked again on every page. This one is set either way, because it is the record of your choice.
  • redux_visitor — a random ID plus the first page you landed on and which site sent you. It contains no name, email or address. Kept for six months.
  • redux_journey — a short list of the pages and buttons you used on this site, so we can see which parts of it are actually helping people. Kept for one month.
  • Google Analytics — if analytics is switched on, Google sets its own cookies to count visits and understand which pages people find useful. Google's own notice explains what it stores.

If you press Decline, none of the tracking cookies are written, anything already stored is removed, and the site works exactly the same. You can also clear cookies at any time in your browser settings.

The contact form

Our contact form is provided by Buildertrend, the construction management software we use to run projects. What you submit goes into our Buildertrend account so it reaches the right person and nothing gets lost between an email inbox and a job site.

Getting in touch

If you want to know what we hold about you, or want it removed, email reduxllcdesigns@gmail.com or call (410) 299-5938 and we will take care of it.
