Remodeling on The Hill

Capitol Hill is a neighborhood of narrow rowhouses, many of them a century old or more, most of them sharing party walls with the house next door. That changes almost every part of a remodel — how material gets in, where a wall can move, what the plumbing and electrical behind the plaster look like, and what a permit application has to show. Redux LLC has spent more than twenty years remodeling homes across Washington DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia, and we plan Hill projects around those realities from the first visit rather than discovering them halfway through.

Kitchen Remodeling in Capitol Hill

Hill kitchens are usually long and narrow, tucked at the back of the house with the only exterior wall at one end. We work out early whether the sink and range can stay where they are — that single decision moves a kitchen budget more than any finish selection — and design cabinetry to the inch, because on the Hill there is rarely a spare one.
